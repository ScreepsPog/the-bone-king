# Army groups and the shaking at the leash (2026-11-12 11:40 PST to 12:10 PST)

## When

2026-11-12 11:40 PST to 12:10 PST.

## Asked

The director played the build in Brave (about 11:40 PST) and reported:

- 120 fps at 4K. Movement and aim are all right for now; aim and Bolt firing get refined with the other abilities.
- The text is good.
- "When a red cube enemy gets to the edge of the yellow ring, the army can meet them at that exact point and cause
  there to be some type of jitter."
- "I like the idea of them clumping into groups, kind of like a wall protecting the player... just occasionally
  there will be a few guys by themselves."
- The red cubes stay as the weak opening enemy. How the army is created should be rethought: (1) kills give
  components and a skeleton is assembled at a threshold, or (2) kills drop specific parts (skull, arm, legs) at random.

## What the agents did

Claude Code (Claude Fable 5.2) found the cause, wrote two tests that failed on the old code, fixed it, packaged the
web build and checked it in the browser pane. Commit `4b4319b`.

- **The shaking.** A skeleton attacked an enemy only inside the leash (500 units). An enemy standing right on that
  line was inside on one frame and outside on the next, so the skeleton ran at it, ran back, and ran at it again
  without ever landing a hit. Fix: a skeleton that is already fighting keeps its fight 80 units past both limits
  (`LeashSlack`).
- **Groups.** A skeleton with no other skeleton within 160 units walks over to the nearest one. One in a group only
  shuffles up to 70 units from its spot, so groups shift but hold (`GroupRadius`, `GroupShuffle`; `GroupRadius 0`
  switches groups off). Before, every skeleton picked a random new spot every few seconds, which is what pulled
  single skeletons out of the groups.
- **The banner.** The engine page's "plays best in Chrome" line is switched off (`OtherBrowserAdvice=none`).
- The page's real-pixel setting now comes from the project settings; the staged page shows it.

Tests: the first new test failed on the old code with "the skeleton backed away 40 times" and the grunt alive; the
second with 6 skeletons standing alone. 38 tests pass now, plus the wall scenario.
